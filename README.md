

# I’m tired of this. Let me be blunt.
I’m angry, and I’m deeply disappointed in this field. wuwuwuwuw

1. How long do we have to walk on eggshells around **AI replacement** narratives?
2. Why should the work we poured real effort into be compared unfavorably to AI slop made in just 10 seconds? 
3. Just because someone is a beginner right now, does that mean they’re a beginner forever?
4. Does a discipline have any future when its own specialists are treated like they’re inferior?
5. Just because a new abstraction tool showed up, does that make foundational knowledge useless?
6. Putting everything else aside, why are we expected to grind ourselves down for “social productivity”? If productivity is all that matters, the learning process itself is inefficient—so why do we even bother with education or academic degrees?
7. Why should we join the panic over far-future AI technology when we can’t even fix the blatant inefficiencies right in front of us? They’ve been screaming nonstop about human jobs being replaced, yet what concrete protections have they actually put in place against job displacement so far? Haven't every single country and institution just been stoking AI fearmongering while sitting back and eating popcorn? Then, why the hell do we need "AI ethics" or "AI philosophy" right now? It’s a complete waste of time—drop the shit immediately.
8. This is strictly an engineering discipline, one so sensitive that even a trivial piece of code breaks over a tiny operational slip or design flaw. If you view this as mere "code monkeying," you know nothing about this field.
9. Professors, experts—whoever you are in computer science—wake up. If code-level skill is supposedly worthless, how do you explain the fact that right now, without even that "trivial" boilerplate AI code, nothing functions at all?
10. Why do voices only get heard when people are furious and at their breaking point? Am I supposed to act like an agitator? Does the world want another Hitler?
11. I’m tired of this. If possible, I want people in engineering to actually stand up against this nonsense. Merely "voluntarily using less AI slop" or offering mild criticism isn't nearly enough. We need concrete ways to protect both specialists and beginners. 
This isn't agitation. Developers should have prepared and taken action for what the AI era would bring. Failing to do so is entirely on the developers and experts who fixated solely on automation, AI hype, productivity, and money—or who bought into collective AI anxiety. In an abnormal environment like this, weeding out beginners just to survive the competition alone isn't something to brag about. It is a shameful, disgraceful stain on us as engineers.

12. Frankly, this whole situation feels like a bad joke or a comedy sketch. What more is there to say? They claim writing AI prompts takes no expertise, while simultaneously claiming coding is useless because AI does it all. Then what the hell does "expertise" mean to them? Paying $150 for Claude just to build a KAWAII maid robot chan? Even then, if someone's just going to say "AI did it", where is the expertise? It’s not the AI that’s hallucinating—it’s these people. People whose judgment has been paralyzed by AI, completely unaware of the nonsense they’re spouting, obsessed only with mockery and belittlement.

13. Thoughtful developers need to stop preaching doom and obsolescence. Focus on sharpening your own craft, and actively mentor and lift up the juniors struggling to break into this field. Since I never got that kind of support, I’m determined to give others what I wished I had received. There is zero expertise in belittling human beings out of sheer fear for your own paycheck, just because one piece of technology happens to be impressive. A developer who has abandoned their humanity is no longer a developer.

14. I just hope we return to a world where I don’t even have to write garbage like this anymore.

Let me be clear: I can live with my own skills being dogwater, but I don't want to tolerate contempt for my experience or my career.
I have spent at least ten years learning programming as if my life depended on it. My work may still be lacking, but there is zero justification for dismissing it simply for lacking flashy deliverables or high output.

I’m not the only one who has prepared and bled for this craft. These people carry undeniable expertise and conviction within their own respective domains. Not even the most "professional, hotshot" developers have the right to casually look down on that.

And even if someone is churning out literal AI-slop or human-slop garbage, there is no such thing as an effortless path. What is the point of an era that applauds AI slop while failing to protect the code written by a beginner? 
Snap out of this "AI, AI, AI" schizophrenia/psychosis, and take a hard look at whether you even have the ability left to write your own code.
